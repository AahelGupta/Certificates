## 🔗 How to Add Certificates to Your LinkedIn Profile

LinkedIn allows adding certifications directly to the **Licenses & Certifications** section of your profile.
With the links above:

1. Click any **Add to Profile** button above or in [index.html](./index.html).
2. LinkedIn will automatically open the **"Add certification"** modal pre-filled with:
   - **Certification Name** (e.g. *Website Design and Development Internship*, *Level 1: Python*)
   - **Issuing Organization** (*Internship Studio*)
   - **Issue Date** (*November 2026*)
   - **Credential ID** (*3625084* / *ISWDI3625084*)
   - **Credential URL** (Points to the verified certificate PDF hosted in this repository)
3. Click **Save** on LinkedIn, and the certification will instantly appear on your public profile!
